About Us

Who We Are

Established in 1996 and headquartered in Johor Bahru, Malaysia, Ayoyo Products (M) Sdn. Bhd. is a trusted manufacturer and distributor of confectionery, snack foods, and beverage products. With nearly three decades of industry experience, we are committed to delivering quality products that bring joy to children and families, creating delightful experiences and cherished childhood memories across Malaysia and beyond.

We take pride in delivering tasty, affordable, and wholesome snacks that delight consumers across the region. From our factory to your table, quality and freshness are our top priorities.

Why Partner With Us

✅ Trusted Certifications

JAKIM Halal-certified and MESTI-certified.

✅ Reliable Supply

Our well-equipped factory enables consistent, large-scale production to meet your demand reliably and on time.

✅ Competitive Pricing

Direct-from-manufacturer pricing gives you better margins while keeping your products affordable for customers.

✅ Customization Available

We welcome OEM and private-label partnerships to bring your unique snack concepts to life.
