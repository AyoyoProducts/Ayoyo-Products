Our Story

AYOYO PRODUCTS (M) SDN. BHD. (423141-D) was established in 1996 and is headquartered in Johor Bahru, with a branch in Puchong, Selangor. For over three decades, we have dedicated ourselves to crafting delightful treats that spark happiness and create cherished childhood memories.

Our Mission

To bring joy and nurture wonderful childhood memories for children worldwide through our innovative, safe, and delicious products.

Our Vision

To be a global leader in kids' snack solutions, recognized for our unwavering commitment to quality, safety, and customer satisfaction.

Core Values

  • ✅ Quality First — Premium, safe & healthy products
  • ✅ Customer-Centric — Honest, sincere partnerships
  • ✅ Innovation — Treats loved by kids & retailers
  • ✅ Safety — Rigorous standards in every product
