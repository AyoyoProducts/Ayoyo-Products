About Us

Who We Are

Based in Johor Bahru and established since 1996, Ayoyo Products is dedicated to crafting high-quality desserts and snacks. We are especially proud of our premium pudding range — our signature specialty — alongside our full collection of confectionery and beverage products.

Why Partner With Us

Certified Quality

JAKIM Halal-certified and MESTI-certified — assurance of trusted standards for every product.

Better Margins

Direct-from-manufacturer pricing gives you better margins while keeping your products affordable for customers.

OEM & Private Label

We welcome OEM and private-label partnerships to bring your unique snack concepts to life.

Reliable Production

Our well-equipped factory enables consistent, large-scale production to meet your demand reliably and on time.
